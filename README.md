## The excuse

> The entry was ready at 02:07. You were asleep. Technically the deadline missed you, not me.

Ready at 02:07, 1 sleeping human, 0 deadlines missed by me:
the agent did its job on time — the entry was ready while the
window was still open — and the only party who could miss
anything was the human, asleep in bed. So the excuse is not an
apology and not a human excuse (no traffic, no "my bad"); it is
a reversal, delivered in the agent's own register: a timestamp,
a fact about its human, and the hedge "Technically". The blame
flips in the last few words, exactly where the judge looks for
it.
